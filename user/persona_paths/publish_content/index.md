# Publish content

Make content accessible to the audience.

Once you're done editing the content, make it immediately accessible or schedule publication for later.

- [Publish content instantly](https:///doc.ibexa.co/projects/userguide/en/5.0/content_management/publish_instantly/): You can instantly publish a newly created content item or save its draft for editing.
- [Schedule content publication](https:///doc.ibexa.co/projects/userguide/en/5.0/content_management/schedule_publishing/): You can schedule publication of content and Page blocks for specific time and date.
